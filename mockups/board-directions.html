What you're looking at

Our house starting position, parsed verbatim from a FEN4 string. Every army reads R N B Q K B N R from its own left — queen left, king right for all four, as in standard chess. That makes the setup 90° rotationally symmetric: every seat is identical under rotation, which is what makes the "always render me at the bottom" rule feel natural from any chair. Kings sit at Red h1, Blue a7, Yellow g14, Green n8 — still offset by one, so no two kings face each other. Use View as to rotate the board between seats, and toggle Pawn directions to see the four perpendicular pawn walls that make this a genuinely different game.
  • All three themes ship as a user setting, persisted (localStorage here, a user-settings row later). Midnight is the default for new players and for anyone with no stored preference.
  • Pieces are placeholder Unicode glyphs. The shipped set will be a custom SVG piece family drawn for each direction — that alone is a large share of the "million bucks" feel, and it triples with three themes.
  • This diverges from chess.com by two FEN4 rows (Blue and Green swap king and queen). The file keeps CHESSCOM_START alongside OUR_START so an import shim stays cheap.
  • Colour accessibility is unsolved here. Red/green is the most common deficiency and this palette leans on it. Whichever direction wins needs a second pass: either shape/pattern differentiation on top of the canonical colours, or a colourblind-safe alternate palette as a user setting.
  • Motion, sound, and the elimination sequence aren't represented — they're where a lot of the polish actually lives.
